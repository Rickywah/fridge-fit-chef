## Global Constraints

- BMR: Mifflin–St Jeor (male `+5`, female `−161`).
- Activity factors: rest `1.375`, medium `1.55`, high `1.725`.
- Goal ratios: cut `0.80`, maintain `1.00`, bulk `1.10`.
- Floor: `targetKcal = max(round(TDEE×ratio), round(BMR×1.1))`.
- Protein table and fat `0.9 g/kg` (fallback `0.7`) exactly as in the spec.
- Display `kcal` = `P×4+C×4+F×9` after macros are set.
- `MEAL_RATIO` stays `0.40`.
- Default `settings.goal = 'maintain'`; missing goal on load → maintain.
- Do not change nutrition100 / servingScale / deduct logic.
